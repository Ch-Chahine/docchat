Chapter 4: The Water Cycle

The water cycle describes how water moves through Earth's systems. It has four main stages.

1. Evaporation: the Sun heats water in oceans, lakes and rivers, turning it into water vapour that rises into the air. About 86% of global evaporation comes from the oceans.
2. Condensation: as the vapour rises, it cools and condenses into tiny droplets, forming clouds.
3. Precipitation: when droplets combine and grow heavy, they fall as rain, snow, sleet or hail.
4. Collection: water gathers in oceans, lakes, rivers and underground aquifers, and the cycle begins again.

Transpiration is the release of water vapour from plant leaves. Together, evaporation and transpiration are called evapotranspiration.
